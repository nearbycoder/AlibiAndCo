Everything in *Alibi & Co.* that isn't listed here was made for this project: the cases and
writing, every 3D model, portrait, press photo and the town map (generated by the Blender scripts
in `ArtSource/`), every texture, icon and newspaper page (generated by the Python scripts in
`Tools/`), and all music, ambience and sound effects (synthesized by `Tools/synth_audio.py`, no
samples).
